SpendWise
Your money, tracked on your phone.
No account. No cloud. No ads. Nothing about your spending is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go.

How it works

{{ m.icon('sms') }}
It reads your bank SMS
Bank and UPI messages already arrive on your phone. SpendWise reads the amount, the merchant and the date out of them, so you do not type anything in.
{{ m.icon('grid') }}
It learns your merchants
Correct a category once and it remembers. The learning happens on this device, from your corrections only.
{{ m.icon('shield') }}
It flags what looks wrong
Messages from senders your bank never uses, or with the shape of a scam, are held aside for you instead of being added silently.

What SpendWise reads

Being specific here matters more than being reassuring, so here is exactly what the SMS permission is used for.
✓ Messages from bank and UPI sender IDs — the amount, merchant, date and reference number in them.
✓ Kept in a database on this phone, which only this app can open.
✗ Never messages from people. Never OTPs.
✗ Never uploaded, never analysed off the device, never shared — there is no server and no analytics in this app at all.
